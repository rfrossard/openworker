# Presentation quality gate

## Narrative

- The audience and desired outcome are explicit.
- Each slide has one job and advances the prior slide.
- Titles express conclusions or takeaways, not generic topics.
- The conclusion resolves the opening question.

## Visuals

- Imagery is relevant, legible, consistently art-directed, and properly captioned.
- No image is stretched, blurry, misleading, or reused without purpose.
- Charts use verified data and label units, period, and source.
- Slides are compositions, not repetitive dashboard-card grids.

## Typography and layout

- Deck title is at least 40 pt; slide titles are at least 28 pt.
- Body copy remains readable and does not overflow.
- No title wraps unexpectedly; no objects clip or overlap.
- Contrast and whitespace are sufficient in both light and dark source images.

## Evidence and files

- Every non-trivial factual claim maps to a source URL.
- Externally sourced images include origin and license when available.
- PPTX begins with the ZIP/OOXML signature and opens as an editable deck.
- PDF begins with `%PDF-`, has the same page count as the PPTX slide count, and shows
  the same slide structure and images.
- Storyboard, source manifest, and claim ledger exist beside the deck.
